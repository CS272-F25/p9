About Cooking Portal

Our Mission

Cooking Portal is a one stop shop where recipes and shopping come together. Our goal is to help home cooks discover new meals, save their favorites, and generate grocery lists based on selected recipes. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned chef, we make meal planning simple and enjoyable.

Features

Recipe Search
Search thousands of recipes from around the world using TheMealDB database.
Browse by Cuisine
Explore recipes organized by cuisine type like Italian, Mexican, Japanese, and more.
Save Favorites
Keep track of recipes you love and access them anytime from your favorites list.
Shopping Cart
Add ingredients directly to your cart and take it with you to the grocery store.
